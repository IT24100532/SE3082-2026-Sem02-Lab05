How master works:
The block under "master" is run only by the master thread, which is always thread 0 (the
thread that started the parallel region). The other threads skip it. The output always
shows "Inside Master # 0".
Unlike single, master has NO hidden barrier at the end. The other threads do not wait for
the master block. That is why threads 3, 2 and 1 print "Hi again" before the master block
has even run.
Difference from single: single = any one thread + waiting at the end;
master = always thread 0 + no waiting.
